Pricing FAQ

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. You can switch between Free and Pro at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking. All payments are securely processed.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

The Free plan is available indefinitely. Try it out and upgrade to Pro whenever you're ready.

What happens when I exceed the daily limit?

On Free, you'll receive a 429 error after 250 requests. Upgrade to Pro for 50,000 requests/day.
